Welcome to Yatra! 🌍

Yatra connects travelers who need assistance with volunteers willing to help during their journeys. Whether you're seeking help or offering support, our platform makes travel more accessible for everyone.

🙋 Need Help?

Register as a Seeker to find volunteers who can assist you during your travels.

  • ✓ Find travel companions on your route
  • ✓ Get help with navigation and immigration
  • ✓ Language assistance and support

🤝 Want to Help?

Register as a Volunteer to offer assistance to travelers on your routes.

  • ✓ Help fellow travelers during your journey
  • ✓ Offer language and navigation support
  • ✓ Build community connections

API Endpoints

Method Endpoint Description
POST /api/auth/google Login/Register with Google OAuth
POST /api/requests/seek Create a seek request
POST /api/requests/volunteer Create a volunteer request
GET /api/matches/discover Discover matches for your requests
GET /api/calendar/ View your calendar events
POST /api/matches/{id}/accept Accept a match (volunteers)

Getting Started

  1. 1
    Authenticate with Google OAuth

    Use the /api/auth/google endpoint to register or login.

  2. 2
    Create a Request

    Post your travel details as a seek or volunteer request with flight information.

  3. 3
    Discover Matches

    Our algorithm will find compatible travelers and notify you via email.

  4. 4
    Accept Match & Connect

    Volunteers accept matches, and contact details are securely exchanged.

Technical Details

Technology Stack

  • ✓ Backend: FastAPI (Python 3.11)
  • ✓ Database: SQLite (development)
  • ✓ Authentication: Google OAuth + JWT
  • ✓ API Docs: Swagger/OpenAPI
  • ✓ Email: SMTP Notifications

Matching Algorithm

  • • Flight Number: 30 points (exact match)
  • • Route Match: 40 points (same airports)
  • • Time Proximity: 30 points (±4 hours)
  • • Language: 10 points (compatibility)
  • • Categories: 10 points (assistance types)

Minimum compatibility score: 50/100
